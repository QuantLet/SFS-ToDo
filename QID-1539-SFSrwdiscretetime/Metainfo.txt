Name of QuantLet: SFSrwdiscretetime

Published in: 'Statistics of Financial Markets : Exercises and Solutions'

Description: 'Python conversion of the original MATLAB QuantLet SFSrwdiscretetime, implemented in SFSrwdiscretetime.py for Spyder using NumPy and Matplotlib. Simulates a geometric Brownian motion stock price over one year with expected return miu = 0.11, volatility sigma = 0.2 and initial price m = 49. Compares daily sampling (250 trading days, upper panel) with hourly sampling (2000 trading hours, lower panel). Both panels use the same Brownian path, sampled every eight trading hours for the daily series, and include the initial price at time zero. Uses the exact GBM solution, displays the figure and exports a transparent PNG.'
